Turret Stars Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Turret Stars ("the game") is made by Finn Paster. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (nights survived, coins, buildings, skins, trophies and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us.

Gameplay statistics

To improve the game, it sends anonymous play statistics to PostHog (posthog.com), an analytics service: for example when a session starts and ends, which night you reach, tutorial progress, which ads were shown and which items were bought. These are tied only to a random ID created on your device, never to your name, email or Apple ID. Some players are randomly given slightly different game settings (such as difficulty) so we can learn which version is more fun. You can turn statistics off any time in the game's Settings under "Share play stats".

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. If you are in a region where consent is required, the game asks for it first. On iPhone, the game asks for permission before any tracking; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the game which items you own so they can be unlocked and restored.

Game updates

The game downloads new game content from the internet when available. This request does not include any personal information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can change tracking permission at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier in Settings. Deleting the game removes all of its saved data from your device.
